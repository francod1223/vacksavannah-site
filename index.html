Our story

Made by people who love this city.

Vack Savannah began with Spencer & Xavi Pepe — lifelong travelers and lovers of real estate who fell for Savannah's historic beauty. Drawn back to the city year after year, they eventually made it home.

One by one, they set out to restore its houses — preserving the heart-pine floors, tall ceilings, and old-world character, while making every space genuinely beautiful to live in. Each home in the collection is one they've poured that care into.

— Spencer & Xavi
