About this home

From the manicured patio to the supple bed linens, we have worked hard to make this place the perfect home away from home. Tucked in the heart of historic downtown Savannah, this home still feels like a cozy escape. The house has been newly renovated and decorated to highlight its historic features like artistic crown molding and timeless hardwood floors. We have all the modern amenities such as central air and a fully stocked kitchen, with added details to make your stay extra special. Our house, built in the 1800s, has been remodeled to include modern amenities while still highlighting its historical features. The house is huge! Three floors with six bedrooms, five baths, two living rooms, two kitchens, and a dining room. Outdoor space includes a front porch, back porch, front and back decks, and a sprawling back patio. The first floor has a living area in the front with huge French doors opening onto the picturesque front porch. The kitchen is fully stocked, and when we say fully stocked, what we mean is that we have all of the cookware, utensils, and appliances that we would want in our own kitchen. In the back of the house, you will find two bathrooms and two bedrooms, and access to the back porch. The bedrooms were designed with comfort in mind: big beds that you can fall right into, ample pillows and thoughtful places for you to keep your things. In the bathrooms, we've gone the extra mile to cover all of your individual needs. The second floor hosts another living room, another fully stocked kitchen, and a gorgeous dining room, as well as two more bedrooms and two more bathrooms. The second floor rounds out with access to furnished front and back decks. The top floor holds two more bedrooms and a bathroom. With stunning views of the sprawling back patio and beautiful light coming from three gorgeous skylights, the third floor further highlights the beauty of this huge historic house. We've set up the house so that it is comfortable, beautiful, and covers all of your needs and more! If there is anything else you need, we will help you find it. We want our property to feel like your home away from home and the perfect southern escape!
