About this home

From the manicured patio to the supple bed linens, we have worked hard to make this place the perfect home away from home. Tucked in the heart of historic downtown Savannah, this home still feels like a cozy escape. The house has been newly renovated and decorated to highlight its historic features like artistic crown moulding and timeless hardwood floors. We have all the modern amenities such as central air and a fully stocked kitchen with added details to make your stay extra special. Our house, built in the 1800's, has been remodeled to include modern amenities while still highlighting its historical features. The home is conveniently all on one floor. The living area is in the front with huge french doors opening onto the sprawling front porch. The kitchen is fully stocked, and when we say fully stocked, what we mean is that we have all of the cookware, utensils and appliances that we would want in our own kitchen. In the back of the house you will find both bathrooms and both bedrooms. The bedrooms were designed with comfort in mind: big beds that you can fall right into, ample pillows and thoughtful places for you to keep your things. In the bathrooms, we've gone the extra mile to cover all of your individual needs. The back of the house opens onto another furnished deck which overlooks the huge, green, manicured patio. We've set up the house so that it is comfortable, beautiful and covers all of your needs and more! If there is anything else you need, we will help you find it. We want our property to feel like your home away from home and the perfect southern escape!
