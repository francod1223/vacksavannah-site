About this home

This charming brownstone is steps from a park and a short walk to Broughton Street, which offers great shopping, dining, and nightlife. The home features large bedrooms and bathrooms, a fully stocked open kitchen, and an inviting living space designed for your comfort. You'll have exclusive access to the entire house, including a lovely courtyard and designated parking. With three bathrooms (two full, one half), a master suite complete with a tub and steam shower, and bright, spacious rooms throughout, this recently renovated home is perfect for your stay. Located in Historic Downtown Savannah, you'll find most attractions, shops, and restaurants within walking distance. Forsyth Park is just three blocks away, and a grocery store is two blocks from the house. For added convenience, street parking and garages are available, and we recommend utilizing the free shuttle service that runs every 10 minutes to explore downtown. We're always just a call or message away to ensure you have a fantastic experience!
