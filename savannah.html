The city

Explore Savannah

Our homes sit in the heart of the Historic District — here's the Savannah we'd send our own friends to.

Where to eat

The Grey

Celebrated Southern dining in a restored Greyhound station.

The Olde Pink House

Classic Savannah fine dining in an 18th-century mansion.

Mrs. Wilkes' Dining Room

Legendary family-style Southern lunch (cash, worth the line).

The Collins Quarter

Buzzy Australian-inspired brunch and coffee.

Leopold's Ice Cream

A 100-year-old institution — don't skip it.

Zunzi's

Cult-favorite sandwiches, a short walk away.

Coffee & mornings

The Sentient Bean

Right on Forsyth Park — the local morning ritual.

The Coffee Fox

Broughton Street favorite for a proper pour.

Foxy Loxy Café

Tex-Mex café in a cozy historic house on Bull Street.

Gallery Espresso

Old-Savannah coffeehouse on Chippewa Square.

What to see

Forsyth Park

The iconic fountain and green heart of downtown — steps from the homes.

The 22 Squares

Savannah's famous oak-shaded garden squares, made for wandering.

River Street

Cobblestone riverfront with shops, restaurants, and history.

Bonaventure Cemetery

Hauntingly beautiful live oaks and Spanish moss.

Wormsloe Historic Site

The unforgettable oak-lined avenue, a short drive out.

Tybee Island

Savannah's beach — about 25–40 minutes via US-80 E.

Getting around

On foot

The Historic District is wonderfully walkable — most everything is close.

Free DOT shuttle & ferry

Downtown loop shuttle plus a free ferry across the river.

To-go cups

Savannah allows open containers downtown — a local rite of passage.

Airport

Savannah/Hilton Head (SAV) is about 15–20 minutes away.

Time your visit

St. Patrick's Day (March)

One of the largest celebrations in the U.S. — book early.

Savannah Music Festival (spring)

Weeks of concerts across the city.

Fall & the holidays

Mild weather, festive squares, and the SCAD Film Festival.
